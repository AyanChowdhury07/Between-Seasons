Game is playable on itch.io! https://slypii.itch.io/between-seasons

Representing the WORLDCHANGING UofG game jam! 

Vane the chicken showcases his abilities by collecting the 4 cloud medals in this retro-style 3d platformer.



Controls:

W/up arrow - Move forward

A/left arrow - Move left

D/right arrow - Move right

S/down arrow - Move down
By manipulating the 4 seasons, vane has different abilities!

By pressing the numbers on the keyboard to change the weather:

1: Spring - Create a branch to cling on to walls (only works in 2 directions)

2: Winter - Sneeze in order to jump high (similar to a rocket) - You may find ice forming in some parts of the map too!

3: Summer - Harness solar power to dash forward. This power is enough to push solar blocks.

4: Autumn - Control the wind below to float in the air easier

Press 'O' to perform each action. (Spring must be against a wall)

Open the cave to collect the biggest cloud medal, by absorbing the two solar blocks beside it (not on top)
